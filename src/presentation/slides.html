01 • THE PROBLEM & GENESIS

Why Did I Make This?

Traditional dormitory fire safety exists primarily as static, ignored paper flyers taped onto noticeboards or dense PDF drills that students glance at once and immediately forget.

📄

The Passive Safety Trap

Under real emergencies (3:00 AM alarm, pitch-black corridors, suffocating smoke), human recall of static text drops by over 80%. Students panic and make fatal mistakes like rushing into elevator shafts or throwing water onto grease fires.

🎮

Interactive Muscle Memory

By translating key procedures into tactile animations—such as feeling door handle heat with the back of the hand, low-smoke 30-60cm crawling, and full 360° Stop-Drop-and-Roll—the human brain forms vivid visual-spatial instincts before a real fire strikes.

🗺️

Architectural Ground Truth

Generic evacuation maps confuse residents. We faithfully replicated the real hollow-courtyard floorplan of Ying-Xi Dormitory 4F, clarifying that the void lightwell cannot be crossed and routing students away from elevator shafts to true stair exits.

🇹🇼

Localized Emergency Readiness

Generic templates teach foreign emergency numbers (911). Our system grounds every protocol directly in Taiwan's emergency infrastructure (119 fire/ambulance, 110 police, and 112 global cellular SOS).

04 • RIGOROUS VERIFICATION & AUDIT

What Did I Check? QA & Safety Audit

In safety-critical applications, misinformation can endanger lives. Here is what was audited and verified:

✅ RESOLVED

Responsive Layout Collapse (800×654)

Fixed flex squeezing where instruction badges compressed text into 1-character columns. Implemented flex-wrap: wrap and fluid flex basis.

✅ RESOLVED

P.A.S.S. Dynamic Language Re-translation

Resolved stale Chinese text on active extinguisher steps by re-evaluating active step data and button states dynamically within applyLanguage().

✅ RESOLVED

Factual Safety Advice on 112 SOS

Eliminated unsafe "works with zero signal" claim. Corrected advice: 112 does not require a SIM card or active carrier, but does require cellular tower coverage.

✅ RESOLVED

Elevator Egress Pathing Correction

Evacuee simulation previously walked into the lift box. Re-targeted coordinates directly into North Stairs (234, 28) and South Stairs (370, 417).

✅ RESOLVED

Architectural Floorplan Fidelity

Verified physical Ying-Xi 4F photo: confirmed odd numbering on west wing (X31–X43) and omission of X15 on east wing are authentic building features.

✅ RESOLVED

Visual Overlap & Pacing Synchronization

Shifted Stop-Drop-Roll status pills above the character head; widened pill margins so AC notices never clip; synchronized timing to 16 seconds.

05 • ROADMAP & FUTURE EXPANSION

What Is Next? The Future Roadmap

Building upon this solid interactive foundation to create a campus-wide connected life-safety ecosystem.

PHASE 1

📱 PWA & Offline Mobile Deployment

Package the deck into a Progressive Web App (PWA) with Service Worker caching. Allows students to access interactive evacuation maps and offline hotlines even during grid power outages.

PHASE 2

🥽 3D WebGL / Three.js Virtual Walkthrough

Elevate the 2D floorplan into an interactive 3D first-person dormitory escape simulator. Students can practice navigating low smoke and locating fire doors in real-time 3D.

PHASE 3

📡 IoT Smart Smoke Detector Integration

Connect campus IoT sensor networks (MQTT / WebSockets) to the floorplan map. In real drills, zones with triggered sensors will dynamically turn red and route safe paths in real time.

PHASE 4

🏆 Gamified Dorm Leaderboard & Quizzes

Turn dormitory safety audits into an annual inter-floor competition. Floors that complete fire audits and pass scenario drills receive safety awards and recognition.
